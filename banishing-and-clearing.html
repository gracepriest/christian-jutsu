Banishing and Clearing - Complete Guide

A prayer for protection and for clearing negative influence

← Back to Prayers

Overview

The Prayer of Banishing and Clearing is a prayer for protection and for removing negative influence from a space or a person.

We begin by calling on God. You do not have to address Him by His Hebrew or ceremonial names. Because we are reaching out to the different attributes of our Father, you may simply call Him "Father." Yet we can use His ceremonial and sacred names, and we have the right to do so. What matters is that we are calling out to God: petitioning Him and beseeching Him to act according to the covenant we have through Jesus Christ.

It helps greatly to picture what each name means every time you speak it:

  • YHWH (Yod-Heh-Vav-Heh) - Imagine the I AM THAT I AM. This name is personal. As you call it out, you are drawing close to the person of God.
  • El Shaddai - Imagine God as all-powerful in your mind and heart. Consider what an all-powerful God is capable of and what He can do.
  • El Elyon - Imagine God Most High: the almighty God who is in control, the Ruler above every power and principality, high and lifted up above all.
  • Yahweh - Remember the covenant God of Israel, who keeps His covenants and promises, the all-powerful God who guided Israel.

Framework v2.1: Optional stars, light imagery, names, and motions can focus attention. Read Article 22 on abiding and Christian practice.

Step 1: The Crucified Cross

In this section, we remind ourselves of the penalty that was paid for our sin. We visualize ourselves giving our sins to Christ: our sins leaving us and going onto Him on the cross. We remember that He suffered for us, taking our sins upon Himself, and that we are united with Christ.

Begin a mental and physical meditation on Jesus' finished work. The movement is optional: its purpose is to turn attention to Christ's self-giving love and the grace already received in Him.

Visualize Christ on the cross—battered, bruised, and suffering—as you form the cross upon your own body through simple gestures. This tangible reminder helps you internalize the gravity of sin and the magnitude of God's sacrificial love.

This would be a good time to confess any shortcomings or sins and make renunciations before continuing with the prayer.

"Jesus Christ is Lord.
I belong to Jesus Christ.
I have been brought near to God through Christ.
I am joined to Christ, and the Holy Spirit dwells within me.
There is now no condemnation for me in Christ Jesus.
I have been delivered from the authority of darkness and brought into the kingdom of God's beloved Son.
Jesus Christ is Lord over me—spirit, mind, body, and life."

Procedure:

The petition you make is entirely up to you. What follows is a structure, an example of the petitions you can offer as you beseech God.

Speak each name in turn. After each name is spoken, offer a short petition in the name of Jesus.

  1. Speak the name "YHWH," then pray:
    "I call upon You in the name of Jesus. Purify my mind and my body of all negative influences."
  2. Speak the name "EL SHADDAI," then pray:
    "I call upon You in the name of Jesus. Purify my mind, and purify this place."
  3. Speak the name "EL ELYON," then pray:
    "Most High God, I call upon You in the name of Jesus. I ask that You purify me and purify this place. In Jesus' name, banish all evil."
  4. Speak the name "YAHWEH," then pray:
    "Yahweh, covenant God of Israel, I call upon You in the name of Jesus. I ask that You purify and bless this space, and that You purify and bless me."

It is important to remember that you are calling out to your Father and petitioning Him. Here, we are petitioning God to purify and to remove negative influences. The words you use can vary depending on what you are truly asking of God from your heart.

Step 3: Prayer for God's Angels

This step is optional. Here we call on God and ask Him to send forth His angels to protect us: at our right hand and at our left, going before us and coming behind us. You may choose to request certain angels by name. But once again, we are not commanding angels; we are asking God to send His angels.

Two example prayers follow. Use whichever fits your prayer, or pray in your own words.

Version 1: Asking for the archangels by name

"Lord God of Israel, in the name of Your Son Jesus Christ,
I ask You to send forth Archangel Michael, that by his sword and his anointing he may protect me from the demonic powers of the enemy and drive the enemy away.

I ask You to send forth Archangel Gabriel, that by his power he may drive off Satan and every evil influence.

I ask You to send forth Archangel Raphael, that by his power and strength he may drive off every evil and demonic thing.

I ask You to send forth Archangel Uriel, that by his power and strength he may drive off every evil influence."

Version 2: Asking for angels by description

"Lord God of Israel, in the name of Jesus Christ,
I ask You to send forth angels of protection.

In the name of the Lord Jesus, I ask You to send forth angels of healing, angels of cleansing, and angels of banishing, to banish every negative influence."

The archangels named in Version 1:

  • Michael - "Who is like God?" - the warrior archangel
  • Gabriel - "God is my strength" - the messenger angel
  • Raphael - "God heals" - healing and divine guidance
  • Uriel - "God is my light" - wisdom and illumination

Step 4: Final Banishment Prayer

Our delegated authority to drive off evil.

"Most High God, in the Name of
Yeshua HaMashiach (Jesus the Messiah),
by the power of Ruach HaKodesh (the Holy Spirit),
I resist evil and reject lies, fear, and condemnation.
In Jesus' name, I command every unclean spirit to cease its influence."

When to Use This Prayer

  • Daily prayer - clear negative influences from your mind, your body, and your home
  • When you feel fear or uncertainty - call on God for His protection
  • When a space feels heavy or unsettled - ask God to purify your home, a room, or your workplace
  • When moving into a new home or space - ask God to purify it, bless it, and protect it
  • After being around negativity - conflict, hostility, or an oppressive atmosphere
  • When praying for another person - ask God to protect them and remove negative influences from their life

Biblical Foundation

  • Genesis 17:1 - "I am El Shaddai; walk before me faithfully"
  • Psalm 7:17 - "I will give thanks to the LORD because of his righteousness; I will sing the praises of the name of the LORD Most High (El Elyon)."

  • Psalm 91:11-12 - "For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways."
  • Hebrews 1:14 - "Are not all angels ministering spirits sent to serve those who will inherit salvation?"
  • Matthew 18:10 - "See that you do not despise one of these little ones. For I tell you that their angels in heaven always see the face of my Father."

Authority in Jesus' Name:

  • Philippians 2:9-10 - "Therefore God exalted him to the highest place and gave him the name that is above every name"
  • Luke 10:17 - "The seventy-two returned with joy and said, 'Lord, even the demons submit to us in your name.'"
