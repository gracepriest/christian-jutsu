Exorcism Prayer

A Prayer of Surrender and Deliverance in Christ

← Back to Prayers

Begin with Christ's Grace

This guide reads the Exorcism Prayer through our doctrinal framework v2.1:

  • The believer's regenerated spirit is united with Christ and cannot be indwelt by demons
  • Divine condemnation and curse have already been removed in Christ
  • Prayer depends on God; no title, name, formula, or sequence guarantees deliverance
  • Pray with a person's willing participation and the care of a trusted Christian community

Remember: The framework affirms medicine and science. Not all suffering is demonic or caused by personal failure. Read Article 22 on abiding and dependence on God.

Overview

This is a prayer rooted in Christ and divine authority, meant to exorcise evil spirits and deliver a person from evil.

This prayer remembers:

  • God's creative power and sovereignty
  • The many Hebrew covenant names of God
  • Your identity as God's child through Christ
  • Deliverance for a specific person from unclean spirits
  • God's attributes addressed to Him in praise
  • God's care, including the ministers He chooses to send

Understanding the Hebrew Names of God

These names help us reflect on God's character. They are not secret formulas, and exact pronunciation does not control His response. You may use the names or pray simply to the Father through Jesus Christ.

The Complete Deliverance Prayer

Note: Replace "N." with the name of the person who has welcomed prayer. The sections provide a pattern for reflection, not a required formula.

Part 1: Invocation of God's Creative Power

In this section, we call upon God and declare who He is. We call Him by His names, which are also His attributes, and we proclaim that He is righteous and that He distinguishes between the righteous and the wicked.

I call upon You, O LORD, who created heaven and earth,
who made night and day, who formed light and permitted darkness.
You are YHWH (I AM WHO I AM), whom no one has seen at any time, the invisible God.
You are EL ELYON (God Most High); You are EL SHADDAI (God Almighty).
You are righteous in all Your ways.
You distinguish between the righteous and the wicked.
You made male and female.

Part 2: Identity in Christ

In this section, we identify ourselves with Christ.

I am united with Your Son by grace,
and Your Spirit lives within me.
Hear me, O LORD!
I am Your child through Your Son, O LORD, God of Israel,
brought near in Your beloved Christ.

Part 3: First Invocation of Names

Hear me:
YHWH (I AM WHO I AM),
ELOHIM (God),
EL ELYON (God Most High),
ADONAI TZEVAOT (Lord of Hosts),
EL SHADDAI (God Almighty),
EHYEH-ASHER-EHYEH (I AM THAT I AM).
Guide us in truth and bring freedom from every evil influence.

Part 4: Humble Appeal

I call upon You, mighty and unseen God, from a humbled spirit:
O LORD GOD, holy and true, King of kings and Lord of lords.
Holy Eternal One!

Part 5: First Deliverance Declaration

Bring freedom and peace to Your servant, N.;
YHWH SABAOTH (LORD of Armies),
ADONAI (Lord),
ELOHIM (God),
EL RO'I (the God who sees me),
EL RACHUM VE-CHANUN (God merciful and gracious).

Part 6: Second Deliverance Declaration

Mighty God!
Strengthen Your servant, N., in the life already given in Christ:
EL GIBBOR (Mighty God),
YHWH NISSI (the LORD my banner),
YHWH RAPHA (the LORD who heals),
YHWH SHALOM (the LORD is peace).

Part 7: Appeal to Christ

Deliver Your servant, N.:
LORD JESUS CHRIST, Son of the living God,
have mercy and save.
You are Lord of lords.
You are God of all the earth.
You are the One whom the winds and seas obey.
You are the One who made all things by the word of Your mouth.
Lord! King! Master! Helper! Save this soul:
Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, one God, blessed forever.

Part 8: Prayer for God's Ministry

Father, send Your holy ministers as You will.
Surround N. with Your care and with people who will offer wise, faithful support.

Part 9: Adjuration of Evil Spirits in the Name of Jesus

In this section, we use the delegated authority Christ has given us to speak directly to any evil spirit and command it, in the name of Jesus, to leave N. We do not bargain or converse with these spirits; we command them by the authority of Christ alone.

In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth,
who was crucified, who rose from the dead,
and who is seated at the right hand of the Father,
far above every principality and power,
I adjure you, every unclean and evil spirit:
you have no right and no authority over N.
N. is under the authority and protection of the Lord Jesus Christ,
whose blood was shed for them.
I plead the blood of Jesus over N.

By the authority Jesus has given me, I command you to leave N. now.
Leave their mind, their body, and every part of their life.
Release every attachment and every hold you have taken.
Come out of N. and enter them no more.
Go where the Lord Jesus Christ sends you.

Christ has disarmed the powers and authorities and triumphed over them by the cross.
At the name of Jesus every knee must bow.
In the name of Jesus Christ, leave N. now!

Scripture: Mark 9:25, Mark 16:17, Luke 10:19, Acts 16:18, Ephesians 1:20-21, Colossians 2:15, Philippians 2:10, Exodus 12:23

When to Use This Prayer

  • When a spirit is manifesting in a person - reacting violently to prayer or to the name of Jesus, as the spirits did before Jesus (Mark 1:23-26; Mark 5:6-7)
  • When a spirit has attached itself to a person - torment, fear, or compulsion that persists after prayer and renunciation
  • After involvement in the occult - witchcraft, divination, spirit guides, or other forbidden practices, once the person has renounced them
  • When a person asks for deliverance - pray with their willing participation, using their name in place of N.
  • When a stronger spirit resists - continue with prayer and fasting, then pray again (Mark 9:28-29)

How to Pray with Someone

  1. Prepare with Scripture - reflect on Jesus' words and intentions
  2. Ask what support is welcome - include trusted believers with the person's agreement
  3. Use oil only if welcomed - a point of contact in prayer, never an independent source of power
  4. Speak calmly - confidence rests in Christ, not intensity or volume
  5. Maintain humility - listen, preserve the person's dignity, and depend on God
  6. Use the person's name - offer the prayer with their willing participation
  7. Allow space - stop when requested; no visible response is required
  8. Follow up - offer community, practical care, and support for appropriate medical care

Biblical Foundation

  • Exodus 3:14 - "God said to Moses, 'I AM WHO I AM'"
  • Isaiah 43:11-13 - "I, even I, am the LORD, and apart from me there is no savior"
  • Mark 16:17 - "In my name they will drive out demons"
  • Luke 10:19 - "I have given you authority... to overcome all the power of the enemy"
  • Acts 16:18 - "In the name of Jesus Christ I command you to come out of her!"
  • James 4:7 - "Resist the devil, and he will flee from you"
  • 1 John 4:4 - "Greater is he who is in you than he who is in the world"

Keep Grace at the Center

  • Christ is the foundation - authority is received through Him, never earned by performance
  • Address prayer to God - do not seek dialogue, information, or power from spirits
  • Grace remains - failure and doubt do not evict the Holy Spirit or restore condemnation
  • Leave room for mystery - an unanswered prayer does not establish weak faith or personal fault
  • Honor God's identity - praise His attributes without claiming to be Him
  • Care for the whole person - we affirm medicine, science, and ongoing compassionate support

"Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you." - James 4:7
