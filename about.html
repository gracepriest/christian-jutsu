About our church

Our Mission

We help believers rest in Christ's finished work and bring daily life into alignment with His words. Our worship, prayer, study, and practical exercises grow from the grace already given in Jesus Christ.

Our church is a community of prayer, study, and shared Christian practice. These resources support our life together, from everyday devotion to our adapted prayer guides. Each practice is read through our doctrinal framework, version 2.1, with Jesus Christ as the center of Scripture and our life together.

What We Believe

We confess Jesus Christ, the Son of God, crucified and risen. Salvation is God's free gift, received by faith alone in Christ alone, apart from works. Justification is complete at faith. Eternal life is secure, and believers do not return to divine condemnation when they struggle or fail.

Our framework teaches that the regenerated spirit is perfected and permanently united with Christ. Spiritual growth renews the mind and trains the body to express that life. Grace never stops flowing; our attention, fruitfulness, and experience of its benefits may vary. Sin has real personal and social consequences, but it does not restore the divine curse removed in Christ.

Abiding in the Words of Christ

We receive all Scripture as inspired and interpret it in the light of Jesus, the fullest revelation of God. Meditation means returning attentively to His words, considering their meaning, trusting them, and practicing them. Its purpose is to internalize His heart, mind, intentions, and disposition so that His pattern shapes our desires, responses, and actions.

Abiding bears fruit; it does not purchase grace or preserve salvation. Faith receives and participates in God's provision. The Holy Spirit produces spiritual fruit and manifestation; faith never compels God. Read Article 22: Abiding power and Christian practice.

Prayer, Healing, and Spiritual Life

We affirm healing in Christ, spiritual warfare, and the believer's delegated authority. Prayer, praise, anointing oil, Communion, and symbolic gestures are expressions of dependence on God. No prayer formula, sacred object, motion, or mental technique has independent power or guarantees an outcome. We reject magic, divination, and seeking spiritual power outside the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

The framework also affirms medicine and science, recognizes that not all illness or suffering is demonic or caused by personal failure, and leaves room for divine mystery. A person's illness or an unanswered prayer does not mean they have lost salvation. God's sovereignty and grace remain even in suffering.

A Community of Grace

We welcome those exploring Christianity and those who have followed Christ for years. We seek a life of love, forgiveness, service, and mutual care. Good works express faith and have meaningful consequences and rewards; they never earn eternal life. God's correction guides and forms us without condemnation.
