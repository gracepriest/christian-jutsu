# CHRISTIAN JUTSU: A Structured Doctrinal Framework (Version 2.1)



### INTRODUCTION



Our church is centered on grace. We believe that the believer is spiritually perfected in Christ at regeneration and that the Christian life is the process of aligning the mind and body with the perfected spirit through faith, grace, abiding in Christ, Christ-centered meditation, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

We strongly affirm:

* Salvation by faith alone in Christ alone apart from works.


* Eternal security.


* No condemnation for believers.


* The finished work of Christ.


* The indwelling Holy Spirit.


* The reality of spiritual warfare.


* The renewing of the mind.


* Divine healing.


* Covenant participation.


* The necessity of abiding in Christ.


* The internalization of the heart, mind, intentions, and disposition of Jesus Christ through meditation on His words and teachings.



We also teach that grace does not stop flowing, Christ never becomes ineffective, and positional union with Christ never changes, though experiential participation in the life of Christ may vary depending on faith, alignment, and spiritual sensitivity.

---

### ARTICLE 1 – THE NATURE OF GOD



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. God is sovereign in rank and authority.


2. God is the highest ruler over all creation.


3. God has voluntarily chosen to operate relationally through covenant, promise, and faith.


4. God binds Himself to His Word because of His faithfulness and character.


5. God retains sovereign prerogative and divine mystery beyond human understanding.


6. Not all events are mechanically traceable.


7. God governs both natural order and supernatural intervention.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Malachi 3:6 — “For I am the Lord, I change not.”


* Amos 3:7 — God revealing things through prophets.


* Psalm 115:16 — “The earth hath he given to the children of men.”


* James 1:5 — God giving wisdom generously.


* Romans 1:20 — Creation revealing God.


* Job chapters 1–2 — God’s sovereignty and divine mystery.



---

### ARTICLE 2 – SALVATION



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Eternal life is a free gift received by faith alone in Christ alone apart from works.


2. Salvation cannot be earned.


3. Salvation is secure and irreversible.


4. Jesus Christ bore all divine punishment for the believer.


5. The believer can never come under divine condemnation.


6. Justification is complete at the moment of faith.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Ephesians 2:8–9 — Salvation by grace through faith.


* John 3:16 — Eternal life through belief.


* Romans 8:1 — No condemnation in Christ.


* Romans 10:9–10 — Salvation through faith.


* Galatians 2:16 — Not justified by works of the law.


* John 5:24 — Passed from death unto life.


* Titus 3:5 — Not by works of righteousness.



---

### ARTICLE 3 – THE BELIEVER’S IDENTITY



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. At regeneration, the believer’s spirit is morally perfected.


2. The believer is a new creation in Christ.


3. The sin nature is dead.


4. The believer’s spirit cannot be corrupted.


5. Positional union with Christ never changes.


6. The life of Christ permanently remains in the believer.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* 2 Corinthians 5:17 — New creation.


* Ephesians 4:24 — Created in righteousness and true holiness.


* 1 John 4:17 — “As He is, so are we in this world.”


* Romans 6:6 — Old man crucified.


* Colossians 3:3 — Hidden with Christ.


* Hebrews 10:14 — Perfected forever.



---

### ARTICLE 4 – SPIRIT, MIND, AND BODY



**Doctrinal Statement**


We teach a three-layer understanding of the human person.

**Spirit**

* Regenerated.


* Perfected.


* United with Christ.


* Cannot be indwelt by demonic spirits.



**Mind**

* Requires renewal.


* Can host strongholds.


* Can become hardened.


* Must align with Christ’s words.



**Body**

* Retains appetites and old conditioning.


* Can be disciplined and aligned.


* Can manifest varying degrees of spiritual power.



Sanctification is not improving the spirit, but aligning the mind and body with the perfected spirit.

**Supporting Scriptures**

* Romans 12:2 — Renewing of the mind.


* 1 Thessalonians 5:23 — Spirit, soul, and body.


* Romans 8:5–6 — Mind set on Spirit vs flesh.


* John 13:10 — “He who has bathed only needs to wash his feet.”


* James 4:8 — Cleanse your hands and purify your hearts.



---

### ARTICLE 5 – FAITH



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Faith in general is believing something is true.


2. Biblical faith is believing and trusting what God has said.


3. Faith includes resting in God’s promises as fact.


4. Faith does not force God.


5. Faith aligns the believer with what God has already made available.


6. Unbelief is believing contrary to God’s Word.


7. Faith functions as participatory alignment with divine promise.


8. Unbelief and doubt counter faith by introducing fear, which neutralizes the believer's active resistance against the enemy.


9. Doubt does not destroy or remove the power of the Holy Spirit, but it misaligns the believer's focus and imagination.


10. When focus is negative or unaligned with God's Word, the believer is hindered from actively projecting their faith and executing divine promises.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Hebrews 11:1 — Substance of things hoped for.


* Hebrews 11:6 — God rewards those who seek Him.


* Hebrews 4:2 — The promise must be mixed with faith.


* Mark 5:25–34 — Woman with the issue of blood.


* Matthew 14:31 — Peter doubting on the water.


* Mark 9:23–24 — “Help my unbelief.”


* Romans 10:17 — Faith comes by hearing.


* James 4:7 — Resisting the enemy.


* 2 Corinthians 10:5 — Taking imaginations and focus captive.

### ARTICLE 6 – GRACE



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Grace is unmerited favor flowing from union with Christ.


2. Grace does not stop flowing.


3. Christ never becomes ineffective.


4. Positional union never fluctuates.


5. Experiential participation may vary.


6. The believer may fail to walk in grace’s benefits, but grace remains operative.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Romans 5:17 — Abundance of grace.


* Galatians 5:4 — Fallen from grace through law reliance.


* Romans 6:14 — Not under law but under grace.


* John 1:16–17 — Grace and truth through Jesus Christ.

### ARTICLE 7 – LAW OF THE SPIRIT VS LAW OF SIN AND DEATH



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus sets believers free from the law of sin and death.


2. Divine judicial condemnation has been removed.


3. The curse of the law has been removed in Christ.


4. Natural laws still operate.


5. The believer is free from divine curse, not from all natural cause-and-effect.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Romans 8:1–5 — Law of Spirit vs law of sin and death.


* Galatians 3:13 — Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law.


* Romans 6:14 — Under grace, not law.


* Galatians 5:1 — Freedom in Christ.

### ARTICLE 8 – CONSEQUENCES OF SIN



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Sin does not restore condemnation for believers.


2. Sin may produce psychological effects, social consequences, reduced alignment, hardened perception, and natural consequences.


3. Divine curse remains removed.


4. Grace shields from divine condemnation.


5. Christ’s life remains even during failure.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Romans 8:1 — No condemnation.


* Hebrews 3:13 — Hardened by deceitfulness of sin.


* Galatians 6:7–8 — Sowing and reaping.

3. Evil generally produces destructive outcomes.

2. God has made multiple covenants throughout history.


3. Covenant alignment yields covenant promises.


4. The New Covenant in Christ is primary for believers.

### ARTICLE 13 – DEMONIC INFLUENCE



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Demons cannot attach to the regenerated spirit.


2. They may influence mind, body, or subconscious.


3. Sin and misalignment may increase vulnerability.


4. Not all suffering is demonic.


5. Demonic power is subordinate to God.


6. Spiritual forces and demonic entities possess varying degrees of rank, resistance, and power.


7. While believers hold absolute authority in Christ over all enemy forces, combating higher-level spiritual threats requires a corresponding build-up of manifested spiritual power within the practitioner.


8. A believer with a low "charge" of manifested power may experience delays or increased resistance when executing their authority against higher-ranking spirits.

### ARTICLE 20 – FORMS OF SPIRITUAL ATTACK (THE THREAT MATRIX)



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Forbidden Practices: All magic, sorcery, and divination are completely condemned by God. Any dealing with spirits outside of God aligns with forces whose ultimate goal is the deception and destruction of mankind. They cannot be trusted.


2. Low/Common Magic: The manipulation of human thought, will, and negative emotional states to transmit harmful energy or affect the outward world. Because Christ taught that hatred in the heart equates to murder, negative human intentions can project harmful spiritual energy.


3. High/Spiritual (Ceremonial) Magic: Any practice that attempts to contact, invoke, evoke, or petition spiritual entities for aid, advice, or power.


4. Sorcery (Pharmakeia): The physical application of magic where a person uses either low or high magic to enchant or anoint a physical object, granting evil spirits access to the mortal realm through that item.


5. Divination & Necromancy: The attempt to communicate with spirits, guides, or the dead, which grants unauthorized entities access to the practitioner's life and mind.


6. Witchcraft: The general, overarching term for all the practices listed above that attempt to manipulate reality or the spiritual realm outside of God's covenant grace.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Deuteronomy 18:10-12 — Prohibition of divination, sorcery, and necromancy.


* Matthew 5:21-22 — Hatred in the heart equating to murder.


* Galatians 5:19-20 — Witchcraft/sorcery listed as works of the flesh.


* John 10:10 — The thief (Satan) comes only to steal, kill, and destroy.

### ARTICLE 21 – SPIRITUAL WARFARE AND DEFENSIVE TACTICS



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. The Armor of Grace: Spiritual defense begins with a firm foundation in the gospel of grace. The Armor of God protects the believer’s mind and heart from the enemy's primary weapons: lies, doubt, and condemnation.


2. Feeding on the Word: Daily feeding on the teachings of Christ aligns the believer's mind with their perfected spirit, allowing them to partake in the divine promises and nature.


3. Vocal Praise: Vocal praise and gratitude actively build up the practitioner's spiritual charge. By verbally praising God, the believer fills their physical vessel (battery) with His holy anointing.


4. The Believer’s Authority: Believers possess delegated authority from Christ. Practitioners do not merely petition God to stop an attack; they speak directly to demonic entities, physical symptoms, and curses, commanding them to cease in the name of Jesus.


5. Anointing Oil: Anointing oil serves as a physical point of contact. Applying it actively grants God direct access to minister to a person or object, cutting through demonic blockages and counteracting enemy enchantments.


6. Communion as Alignment: Taking the Lord's Communion functions as an act of participatory alignment. By visualizing Christ's broken body for physical restoration and His shed blood for the remission of sins, the believer thwarts demonic attacks, particularly against the physical body.


7. Pleading the Blood: Pleading the blood of Jesus provides an immediate spiritual shield of grace. Once applied, it spiritually hinders the "destroyer" or "waster" from destroying the believer, offering vital protection even if the practitioner is currently struggling with a vulnerability.



**Supporting Scriptures**

* Ephesians 6:11-15 — Putting on the full armor of God.


* 2 Peter 1:4 — Partakers of the divine nature.


* Psalm 22:3 — God inhabits (inherits) the praises of His people.


* Mark 16:17 — In my name they will drive out demons.


* Matthew 8:8-9 — The Centurion’s understanding of authority.


* James 5:14 — Anointing the sick with oil.


* 1 Corinthians 11:23-26 — Partaking in the Lord's body and blood.


* Exodus 12:23 — The blood protecting from the destroyer.

### ARTICLE 22 – THE PRINCIPLE OF ABIDING POWER, AND THE DISTINCTION BETWEEN GRACE-MANIFESTATION AND MAGIC



**Doctrinal Statement**

1. Abiding power flows from living union with Jesus Christ and the active presence of His words within the believer. The believer does not create divine power but participates in the life, promises, and power already given in Christ.


2. The words and teachings of Christ are to remain in the believer through continual attention, meditation, belief, and practice. As His words abide within the believer, they increasingly shape the believer's perception, desires, imagination, responses, and conduct.


3. Meditation on Christ's words is attentive, faith-filled reflection that allows His meaning and intention to take root in the believer. It is not an attempt to empty the mind, manipulate reality, or gain power independently of God.


4. All Scripture is valuable and inspired, but it must be interpreted in the light of Jesus Christ, who is the fullest revelation of God's character, will, and redemptive purpose. Old Testament events and examples must not be imitated apart from the spirit, teachings, and intentions revealed in Christ.


5. James and John appealed to Elijah's example when they desired to call down fire upon a Samaritan village, but Jesus rebuked them. This demonstrates that a biblical precedent may be misapplied when it is separated from the heart and mission of Christ.


6. Abiding includes continuing in Christ's words and allowing those words to govern the mind, imagination, desires, and actions. Obedience does not purchase grace, maintain justification, or preserve eternal life; rather, it expresses faith and keeps the believer experientially aligned with Christ's intentions.


7. Confidence in prayer is strengthened when the believer's desires are formed by Christ's abiding words. The promise, “ask what you will,” is therefore relational and participatory: the believer asks from within communion with Christ rather than attempting to compel God from outside that relationship.


8. Grace-manifestation is the work of the Holy Spirit flowing from union with Christ and received through faith. Prayer, praise, meditation, anointing, Communion, and the exercise of delegated authority are acts of covenant participation and dependence upon God; they are not mechanisms that possess power independently of Him.


9. Magic seeks to manipulate spiritual forces, hidden laws, objects, symbols, formulas, or spiritual beings in order to produce an effect apart from faithful dependence upon God. Grace-manifestation does not control God, command the Holy Spirit as an impersonal force, or obtain power from any entity outside the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.


10. A Christian practice is not made magical merely because it includes spoken words, physical objects, repeated actions, visualization, or symbolic acts. The decisive distinction is its source, object, authority, intention, and posture: Christian practice trusts God and responds to His grace, whereas magic seeks autonomous control or unauthorized spiritual power.


11. No prayer formula, ritual motion, sacred object, or mental technique guarantees a manifestation. God alone is the source of power, the Holy Spirit is the agent of manifestation, Christ is the covenant foundation, and faith is the believer's participatory response.


12. Positional union with Christ remains permanent even when a believer fails to abide fruitfully. Abiding concerns fellowship, formation, fruitfulness, confidence, and experiential participation; it is not a condition for retaining salvation.


**Meditation on the Words and Teachings of Christ**

We give special attention to the words, teachings, actions, character, and revealed intentions of Jesus Christ. This focus does not reject the remainder of Scripture. Rather, it reads the whole biblical witness through the clearest revelation of God given in the Son. Meditation therefore includes returning to Christ's teachings, holding them in the mind, believing them, considering their meaning, and practicing them until His pattern increasingly shapes the believer's inner and outward life.


**Summary**

The objective of meditation on the words of Christ is not merely the accumulation of biblical knowledge but the internalization of the heart, mind, intentions, and disposition of Jesus Christ. His words are to remain within the believer until the believer increasingly sees, believes, desires, responds, and acts according to the pattern revealed in Christ.


**Supporting Scriptures**

* John 8:31–32 — Continuing in Christ's word as His disciples and knowing the truth.


* 1 John 2:5–6 — God's love perfected in the one who keeps His word and walks as Christ walked.


* 1 John 3:21–24 — Confidence before God, answered prayer, obedience, faith in Christ, love, and mutual abiding through the Spirit.


* Colossians 3:16 — Letting the word of Christ dwell richly within the believer.


* Romans 12:2 — Transformation through the renewing of the mind.


* 2 Corinthians 3:18 — Transformation into Christ's image through beholding Him.


* Hebrews 1:1–3 — God's climactic self-revelation in His Son.


* Luke 24:27 — The Scriptures interpreted in relation to Christ.


* Luke 9:54–56 — James and John corrected for seeking an Elijah-like act against the Samaritan village.


* Acts 8:18–23 — Divine power cannot be purchased or treated as a controllable commodity.


* 1 Corinthians 12:4–11 — Spiritual manifestations are distributed and empowered by the Holy Spirit according to His will.


* John 14:12–14 — Works and prayer grounded in Christ and His name.

### CORE THESIS



The believer is spiritually perfected in Christ. Grace permanently removes divine condemnation. Sanctification is the alignment of mind and body with the perfected spirit. Faith aligns the believer with what Christ has already made available. The Holy Spirit produces manifestation. The believer abides by allowing the words of Christ to remain within and form the heart, mind, intentions, and disposition according to His pattern. Grace-manifestation flows from union with Christ and dependence upon the Holy Spirit; it is not the manipulation of spiritual forces. Natural law remains, but divine curse has been removed. Grace never stops flowing. Positional union remains permanent, while experiential participation varies. Divine mystery remains where Scripture does not fully explain.
