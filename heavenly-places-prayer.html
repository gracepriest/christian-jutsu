Prayer to Banish Negative Influences in the Heavenly Places

Complete Guide

← Back to Prayers

Overview

This prayer uses our delegated authority in Christ to command any enemy operating from the heavenly realms to cease every operation of negativity against us: against our minds, our bodies, and our livelihoods.

Scripture gives many examples of a spirit working against a person to cause delays, sickness, and setbacks:

  • Daniel 10:12-13 - The prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood the answer to Daniel's prayer for twenty-one days.
  • Job 2:7 - Satan struck Job's health, afflicting him with painful sores.
  • Zechariah 3:1-2 - Satan stood at the right hand of Joshua the high priest to resist him. On earth, people were working to stop the rebuilding of the temple, but God showed Zechariah in a vision that a demonic force stood behind that opposition.

This prayer is aimed specifically at spiritual forces in the heavenly places that may be trying to hinder your progress, your health, or your finances; to block your path; or to block what God is doing in your life.

1. Intention (Spoken at the Start)

"Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews,
is Lord of Lords and King of Kings."

2. The Crucified Cross

In this section, we remind ourselves of the penalty that was paid for our sin. We visualize ourselves giving our sins to Christ: our sins leaving us and going onto Him on the cross. We remember that He suffered for us, taking our sins upon Himself, and that we are united with Christ.

Begin a mental and physical meditation on Jesus' finished work. The movement is optional: its purpose is to turn attention to Christ's self-giving love and the grace already received in Him.

Visualize Christ on the cross—battered, bruised, and suffering—as you form the cross upon your own body through simple gestures. This tangible reminder helps you internalize the gravity of sin and the magnitude of God's sacrificial love.

This would be a good time to confess any shortcomings or sins and make renunciations before continuing with the prayer.

"Jesus Christ is Lord.
I belong to Jesus Christ.
I have been brought near to God through Christ.
I am joined to Christ, and the Holy Spirit dwells within me.
There is now no condemnation for me in Christ Jesus.
I have been delivered from the authority of darkness and brought into the kingdom of God's beloved Son.
Jesus Christ is Lord over me—spirit, mind, body, and life."

3. INRI – Reflection on Jesus' Kingship

INRI stands for the Latin Iesus Nazarenus, Rex Iudaeorum: "Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews." Pilate had these words fastened to the cross above Jesus' head, written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek (John 19:19-22). He meant them as the charge against Jesus, but they proclaimed the truth to the whole world: Jesus is King.

Take a moment to remember Jesus as King, reigning far above every power and authority in the heavenly places (Ephesians 1:20-21). Then pray:

3.1 Remember God's Creative Care

"Father, You are the source of life.
By Your Spirit, renew my attention to Christ.
Hold my work and decisions in Your care."

3.2 Kingship and Humble Servant

"Jesus, Your kingdom endures.
You reveal humility and faithful service.
Shape my intentions by Your words."

3.3 Christ the Head and Chief

"Jesus, You are the Head of Your body, the church.
Your authority is above every power.
Let Your love direct my desires and actions."

3.4 Call to Listen and Submit

"Lord, help me listen to Your words,
trust Your grace,
and express my faith in love."

3.5 Yeshua Litany

"Yeshua, Jesus, the Beginning and End, beloved Son of the Father.
You give living water and send the Holy Spirit.
You welcome the burdened and bring freedom.
You are the Light and the Life.
Jesus, crucified and risen, I rest in Your grace."

4. King Above and Below

4.1 Above

"Father, in Christ I am seated with Him in the heavenly places.
Jesus, You reign above every power and every accusation.
Your grace is higher than every curse, every fear, every story told about me."

4.2 Below

"Lord Jesus, all authority on earth has been given to You; the earth and everything in it are Yours.
This ground, this body, this house belong to You by grace."

Key Scriptures

  • Matthew 28:18 - "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me."
  • Ephesians 1:10 - "…to unite all things in him, things in heaven and things on earth."
  • Colossians 1:16 - "For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities—all things were created through him and for him."
  • Philippians 2:10-11 - "…so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord."
  • Revelation 1:5 - "…Jesus Christ the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of kings on earth."
  • Revelation 11:15 - "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and he shall reign forever and ever."
  • Acts 10:36 - "…he is Lord of all."
  • Psalm 24:1 - "The earth is the LORD's and the fullness thereof." (quoted in 1 Corinthians 10:26)

5. Christ Over Every Side of Life

Which image you choose is optional and not important. You may envision the Star of David, a cross, or an image of Christ, or pray with no image at all. What we are doing here is associating ourselves with Christ and using the delegated authority He has given us to command negative spiritual influences to stop all harassment against us.

Why white to blue? This part is also optional. The change of color is symbolic: it pictures moving from the ordinary to the divine, because blue is the color of the divine.

  1. Envision your chosen image in front of you, glowing white
  2. As it forms, see it turn from white to deep blue
  3. As it turns blue, say: "Yeshua, Jesus the Christ, Son of YHWH, is Lord."
  4. Then pray the prayer for that area of life

5.1 Future and New Beginnings

Envision your chosen image → see it turn from white to blue:

"Yeshua, Jesus the Christ, Son of YHWH, is Lord."

Then:

"Lord Jesus, You are Lord over my future and every new beginning.
I reject every story that says my future is cursed or doomed.
In Your name, I refuse any power, principality, rulers and authorities,
and the powers of this world's darkness, spiritual powers of evil in the heavenly places.
I command these to submit to Christ
and stop any attack against me."

5.2 Strength, Actions, Passion, Health

Envision your chosen image → see it turn from white to blue:

"Yeshua, Jesus the Christ, Son of YHWH, is Lord."

Then:

"Lord Jesus, You are Lord over my strength, my actions, and my passion.
In the name of Jesus, I command all power, principality, rulers and authorities,
and the powers of this world's darkness, spiritual powers of evil in the heavenly places.
I command these to submit to Christ
and stop any attack against my strength, health, passion, and my actions."

5.3 Past, Mind, Memory, Emotions

Envision your chosen image → see it turn from white to blue:

"Yeshua, Jesus the Christ, Son of YHWH, is Lord."

Then:

"Lord Jesus, You are Lord over my past and my emotions.
I renounce every accusation rising from my history.
In the name of Jesus, I command all power, principality, rulers and authorities,
and the powers of this world's darkness, spiritual powers of evil in the heavenly places.
I command these to submit to Christ
and stop any attack against my mind, memory, and emotions."

5.4 Unknown, Hidden Things, Night, Soul

Envision your chosen image → see it turn from white to blue:

"Yeshua, Jesus the Christ, Son of YHWH, is Lord."

Then:

"Lord Jesus, You are Lord over what I cannot see or understand.
I renounce superstition, paranoia, and fear of the unknown.
In Your light, the hidden things lose their power to terrify.
In the name of Jesus, I command all power, principality, rulers and authorities,
and the powers of this world's darkness, spiritual powers of evil in the heavenly places.
I command these to submit to Christ
and stop any attack against my soul.
The Spirit lives in me; I am not a victim."

6. Final Centering and Close

"Father, in Christ I am Your child.
I am sustained by Your grace.
No curse, spirit, or failure is stronger than the Cross and Resurrection of Jesus.

Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews,
is Lord of Lords and King of Kings.

Amen."

Biblical Foundation

  • Ephesians 6:10-18 - The full armor of God
  • Philippians 2:9-11 - At the name of Jesus every knee shall bow
  • James 4:7 - Resist the devil and he will flee from you
  • 1 John 4:4 - Greater is He who is in you than he who is in the world
  • Colossians 2:15 - Christ disarmed the powers and authorities
  • Ephesians 1:19-21 - Christ is seated far above all rule and authority

When to Use This Prayer

  • When your progress is delayed or blocked - plans, prayers, or breakthroughs seem held back (Daniel 10:12-13)
  • When your health is under attack - sickness or weakness seems to linger (Job 2:7)
  • When your finances or livelihood are hindered - work, provision, or opportunities meet repeated resistance
  • When opposition rises against what God is doing in your life - your path seems blocked at every turn (Zechariah 3:1-2)
  • When your mind is harassed - fear, accusation, or heaviness comes against your thoughts and emotions

Critical Reminders

This is about GRACE, not works:

  • You are NOT earning protection through this practice
  • Victory comes from Christ's finished work, not your performance
  • You are operating from authority in Christ, not trying to earn it
  • The Spirit forms your mind and conduct; the regenerated spirit is already perfected in Christ
  • You stand here to remember what Christ has done, not to make it happen

This is covenant relationship:

  • The prayers for each area of life can be customized as needed
  • The six-pointed star functions here as optional covenant imagery, with no independent power
  • You are exercising authority already given to you in Christ
  • Every word and gesture points back to the Cross and Resurrection

"For though we walk in the flesh, we are not waging war according to the flesh. For the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but have divine power to destroy strongholds." - 2 Corinthians 10:3-4
