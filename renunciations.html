Renunciations

Turning from sin and walking in the freedom of Jesus Christ.

Return to the Crucified Cross prayer

Welcome to the beginning of freedom! Renouncing is a sacred act of turning away from the things that hold us captive and turning fully toward the love and lordship of Jesus Christ. It is an expression of repentance and a reaffirmation of our identity as children of light. We close the door to sin, resist the devil, and stand on the truth of God's Word.

You do not need to renounce anything you are not actively participating in. Choose the prayers that apply to your present life. You do not need to revisit sins you have already confessed and left behind, or pray every renunciation on this page.

As we turn from sin and deception, we invite the Holy Spirit to fill our hearts with His peace and power and bring healing to the places where we have been hurt. Through these prayers, you are choosing Jesus above every false promise and harmful pattern, and entrusting past wounds to His care.

Your authority comes from Christ alone: His blood, His victory, and His unfailing love. Let this be a moment of release and a new beginning as you move from bondage to blessing. Leave behind your former way of life and remember who you are in Him: a new creation in Christ.

Opening Prayer of Surrender

Heavenly Father, I come before You in the name of Jesus Christ, humbly confessing my sins. I acknowledge the ways I have opened doors to darkness through my actions, words, and thoughts. Where I am participating in harmful patterns passed down through my family, I repent and turn away from them.

Today, I surrender my life completely to You. Wash me clean with the blood of Jesus, renew my mind, and make me whole. I choose to belong to You alone, Lord Jesus. Amen.

Renouncing Witchcraft and Occult Practices

In the name of Jesus Christ, I renounce any witchcraft or occult practices in which I am participating, including sorcery, divination, astrology, fortune-telling, tarot, seeking spirit guides, spells, charms, and curses.

I break every agreement with the kingdom of darkness that I am continuing in, whether I entered it knowingly or unknowingly. I declare that Jesus Christ is my only Lord and Savior. I command every unclean spirit connected to this involvement to leave me now in the name of Jesus.

Holy Spirit, fill every place that was once occupied by darkness with Your presence and power. Amen.

Renouncing Fornication and Sexual Sin

Lord Jesus, I repent of sexual sin in my life, including sexual relations outside marriage, adultery, lust, and impurity. I turn away from these actions and patterns.

I renounce every soul tie, or continuing attachment, formed through sexual immorality that I am holding on to. I break every unholy bond I am maintaining outside Your covenant of marriage.

I declare that my body is the temple of the Holy Spirit, redeemed and sanctified by the blood of Jesus. I receive Your forgiveness, cleansing, and restoration of purity in my spirit, soul, and body. Amen.

Renouncing Drugs and Substance Abuse

Father God, I confess and repent of abusing drugs, alcohol, or any substance that has taken control of my life. I renounce the spirit of addiction, escapism, and bondage. In the name of Jesus, I break every chain of dependency and every curse of destruction over my life.

I declare that my mind belongs to Christ. I choose clarity, peace, and sobriety through the power of the Holy Spirit. Lord, fill me with Your joy and strength instead of the counterfeit comforts of the world. Amen.

Renouncing Abuse (Given and Received)

Being abused is not your sin, and you do not need to repent for what someone else did to you. If you have suffered abuse, you may use the following words as a prayer for healing. The prayer of repentance below applies only if you are harming or abusing others.

A prayer for healing

Lord Jesus, I bring before You every wound caused by physical, emotional, sexual, or spiritual abuse. I renounce the spirit of fear and reject the hold of trauma, rejection, and self-hatred over my life. Help me forgive those who have hurt me and release them into Your hands.

Heal my heart, Lord. Restore my dignity, my voice, and my peace. I receive Your love as my refuge and strength. Amen.

A prayer of repentance for harm I am causing

Lord Jesus, I repent of the ways I am hurting or abusing others. I renounce these actions and choose to turn away from them. Help me take responsibility for the harm I have caused and change how I treat others. Teach me to walk in Your love. Amen.

Closing Prayer of Dedication

Father, I thank You for setting me free. In the name of Jesus, I seal these renunciations with the blood of Christ. Amen.
